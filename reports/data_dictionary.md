# Data Dictionary: mmm_analysis_ready.csv

66 columns, 36 monthly rows (2023-01-01 to 2025-12-01). Generated by `src/preprocessing.py` (function `write_data_dictionary`).

The modelling_role column is guidance for the modelling team. Columns marked `exclude` should not be used as predictors of revenue because they are a result of spend, are built from the target, or are a sum of other columns.
